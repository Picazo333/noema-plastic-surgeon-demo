# Demo Business Plan — VÉRTICE

> All figures are editable fictional assumptions for demonstrating a planning workflow. They are **not market forecasts**.

## Strategic thesis
Build a physician-led elective-surgery practice positioned around discretion, natural-looking outcomes, clear expectations and a high-touch consultation journey. The digital channel should optimize for **qualified consultations**, not maximum raw lead volume.

## Offer architecture
- Core surgical families: face, breast, body and reconstructive consultation.
- Primary conversion: private evaluation request.
- Secondary conversion: procedure education / consultation readiness.
- Explicitly avoid price-discount framing in premium acquisition.

## Target segments (demo)
1. **Considered first-time patient** — high research depth, high anxiety, needs clarity and trust.
2. **Revision / second-opinion patient** — values physician judgment and realistic expectation setting.
3. **Post-pregnancy / body-contouring patient** — values privacy, recovery planning and logistics.

## Funnel model (editable in the public demo)
Default demonstration assumptions:
- 80 qualified leads / month
- 38% consultation booking
- 82% consultation attendance
- 28% attended-consult-to-procedure
- MXN 62,000 average collected revenue / procedure
- MXN 55,000 monthly acquisition + content spend

These numbers exist only so the calculator has a baseline.

## 90-day operating plan
### Days 1–30 — Foundation
- Credential/compliance verification before real publication.
- Photo/video production of real facility and physician.
- Procedure content map and consultation script.
- CRM stages and response SLA.
- Analytics event plan with consent controls.

### Days 31–60 — Acquisition
- Search intent landing variants.
- Organic education cluster by procedure family.
- Paid-search pilot with strict negative keywords and call tracking.
- Lead qualification and consultation-show workflow.

### Days 61–90 — Optimization
- Measure qualified-lead-to-consult, consult show rate and surgery close rate.
- Refine content based on objections heard in consultations.
- Improve response latency and no-show recovery.
- Expand only channels that generate qualified consults, not vanity traffic.

## Core KPIs
- Qualified leads / month
- Lead → consult booked
- Consult show rate
- Consult → procedure
- Cost per qualified consult
- Revenue per acquired procedure
- Review/consent capture rate (real implementation only)
- Response-time SLA

## Growth principle
Do not automate clinical judgment. Automate intake logistics, reminders, content distribution, reporting and non-clinical follow-up while preserving human review where medical or reputational risk exists.
