# Demo Safety & Compliance Boundary

This repository is deliberately designed **not** to function as a real medical intake system.

## Mandatory demo controls
- Fictional physician disclosure visible on every public page.
- No fabricated cédula, board certification or hospital privileges.
- No real patient photos, testimonials or before/after assets.
- No medical diagnosis, candidacy determination or individualized advice.
- No guarantees, superlatives or risk-free language.
- Contact/consultation UI does not submit data anywhere.
- No cookies, trackers, analytics, localStorage, sessionStorage or remote APIs.

## Before a real deployment
A real practice would require jurisdiction-specific legal/compliance review, verified credentials, consented media, privacy notices, secure intake infrastructure, appropriate retention/access controls and medical-review signoff for clinical content.

Noema owns the **project governance/evidence boundary**, not medical or legal truth.
